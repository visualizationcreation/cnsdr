Make a little space

Come as you are.

  1. Get comfortable.

    Choose a place where you can pause. Headphones can help the sound feel close.

  2. Let the story arrive.

    You can follow the pictures, close your eyes, or drift between the two.

  3. Return in your own time.

    Pause whenever you like. Stay with whatever image, sound or feeling lingers.
